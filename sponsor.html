Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about child sponsorship

How long does sponsorship last?

We request a minimum commitment of one year, but many sponsors continue until their child completes their education. You can discontinue at any time with 30 days notice.

Can I communicate with the child I sponsor?

Yes! You can exchange letters, emails, and even schedule video calls. We encourage sponsors to build relationships with their sponsored children while maintaining appropriate boundaries.

How is my donation used?

85% of your donation goes directly to program costs (education, nutrition, healthcare). 10% supports administration, and 5% goes toward fundraising efforts. We provide detailed financial reports annually.

What if the child I sponsor leaves the program?

If a child leaves our program (usually because they've completed their education or their family situation has improved), we'll immediately notify you and help you select another child to sponsor.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about supporting education for underprivileged children

How can I sponsor a child through Lit Kiran?

Sponsoring a child through Lit Kiran is simple. Visit our "Sponsor a Child" page, browse through profiles of children needing support, and select a child you'd like to sponsor. Your monthly contribution will provide educational materials, school fees, uniforms, and other necessary resources to help them succeed in their education.

What percentage of my donation goes directly to the children?

At Lit Kiran, we are committed to transparency. 85% of every donation goes directly to program services that benefit the children, including educational resources, school supplies, and teacher support. 10% goes to administrative costs, and 5% to fundraising efforts to help us reach more children in need.

Can I volunteer with Lit Kiran if I'm not a teacher?

Absolutely! While we welcome teachers, we have various volunteering opportunities for people with different skills. You can help with fundraising events, administrative tasks, digital marketing, content creation, or even mentor children online. Visit our Volunteer page to see current opportunities and apply.

How does Lit Kiran select the children to support?

We partner with local communities, schools, and NGOs to identify children from economically disadvantaged backgrounds who are at risk of dropping out of school. Our selection process considers family income, number of dependents, and the child's enthusiasm for education. We prioritize helping children who show academic potential but lack financial resources.

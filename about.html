Our Mission & Vision

We believe every child deserves access to quality education and opportunities

Our Mission

To provide quality education, healthcare, and life skills to underprivileged children, enabling them to break the cycle of poverty and realize their full potential.

We work directly with communities to create sustainable change through education, empowerment, and opportunity.

Our Vision

We envision a world where every child has access to quality education, healthcare, and the opportunity to pursue their dreams regardless of their socioeconomic background.

A world where communities are empowered to create lasting change from within.

Our Approach

How we work to create lasting change in the communities we serve

Education

Providing quality education through after-school programs, tutoring, and educational resources.

Healthcare

Ensuring children have access to regular health check-ups, nutrition, and medical care.

Community

Working with families and communities to create sustainable change from within.

Empowerment

Teaching life skills and providing vocational training to older children and youth.

Our Journey

From a small initiative to a movement impacting thousands of lives

2010

Lit Kiran was founded by a group of passionate educators who wanted to make a difference in the lives of underprivileged children.

2012

We established our first learning center in a small rented space, serving 35 children from the local community.

2015

Expanded our programs to include healthcare and nutrition support, serving over 200 children across 3 communities.

2018

Launched our vocational training program for older youth, helping them develop skills for future employment.

2020

Despite the challenges of the pandemic, we adapted our programs to provide remote learning and support to our children.

2023

Now serving over 1,200 children across 35 communities with comprehensive education, healthcare, and empowerment programs.

Back to School Drive

October 25, 2025
10:00 AM - 4:00 PM
Central Community Hall, 123 Main Street

Our annual Back to School Drive aims to provide underprivileged children with all the necessary supplies to start their academic year with confidence and enthusiasm.

How You Can Help

  • Donate school supplies (notebooks, pencils, bags, uniforms)
  • Volunteer at the event to help with distribution
  • Sponsor a child's educational kit for ₹150
  • Help spread the word on social media

Event Schedule

  • 10:00 AM - Volunteer briefing
  • 10:30 AM - Doors open for recipients
  • 12:00 PM - Guest speaker: Local education minister
  • 1:00 PM - Lunch break
  • 2:00 PM - Distribution continues
  • 4:00 PM - Event concludes

Health & Nutrition Camp

September 5, 2025
9:00 AM - 5:00 PM
Rural Village Center, 45 Village Road

Our Health & Nutrition Camp provided free medical check-ups, nutritional supplements, and awareness sessions for children and mothers in rural communities.

Event Impact

  • 250+ children received health check-ups
  • 150+ mothers attended nutrition awareness sessions
  • 500+ nutritional supplement packets distributed
  • 15 volunteer doctors participated

Testimonials

"This camp was a blessing for our village. My children received proper medical attention and we learned about nutritious meals we can prepare with locally available ingredients." - Priya, mother of two

Future Plans

Due to the success of this camp, we plan to organize quarterly health camps in different rural areas. Our next camp is scheduled for December 2025.

Upcoming

Digital Literacy Workshop

November 12, 2025
1:00 PM - 5:00 PM
City Library Auditorium, Central Library Building

This workshop aims to introduce underprivileged children to essential digital skills and computer literacy, preparing them for our technology-driven world.

Workshop Content

  • Basic computer operations and terminology
  • Internet safety and responsible usage
  • Introduction to word processing and presentations
  • Basic coding concepts through visual programming
  • Educational games and interactive learning

Requirements

No prior computer knowledge is required. The workshop is designed for children aged 10-16. We provide all necessary equipment, but participants are welcome to bring their own laptops if available.

How You Can Help

  • Volunteer as a tech mentor (basic computer knowledge required)
  • Donate laptops or tablets for continued learning
  • Sponsor internet connectivity for the workshop
  • Help spread awareness about the event

Annual Cultural Festival

11:00 AM - 8:00 PM
Community Grounds, City Park Area

Christmas Joy Celebration

December 15, 2025
2:00 PM - 6:00 PM
Orphanage Center, 67 Hope Street

Spread holiday cheer with underprivileged children through games, gifts, and a special Christmas meal at our annual Christmas Joy Celebration.

Event Activities

  • Christmas carols and festive decorations
  • Gift distribution for 100 children
  • Special Christmas meal with traditional treats
  • Games and activities with Santa Claus
  • Photo booth with festive props

How You Can Help

  • Donate new toys or gifts (unwraped please)
  • Sponsor a child's gift (₹500 per child)
  • Volunteer for event setup and activities
  • Contribute towards the Christmas meal
  • Help with transportation for children

Gift Guidelines

Please ensure gifts are new and unwrapped. Suitable gifts include toys, books, clothing, and educational materials. We welcome gifts for children of all ages (0-18 years).

Education Support Program

Our Education Support Program is designed to remove financial barriers that prevent children from accessing quality education. We believe education is the most powerful tool to break the cycle of poverty.

What We Provide:

School supplies including books, uniforms, and stationery
Tuition fees and exam costs coverage
After-school tutoring and homework assistance
Digital literacy programs with computer access
Scholarships for higher education

Since 2015, we've supported over 5,000 children through this program, with 89% of them showing significant academic improvement within one year of joining.

Impact Story:

Riya, a 12-year-old from a rural community, was able to continue her education through our support after her father's illness made it impossible for her family to afford school expenses. She's now top of her class and dreams of becoming a doctor.

Healthcare & Nutrition Program

Our Healthcare Program ensures that children remain healthy and able to focus on their education. We address both immediate health needs and long-term wellness through preventive care.

Our Services:

Regular health check-ups and medical camps
Nutritionally balanced meals daily
Vaccination drives and immunization programs
Dental and vision care services
Health education for children and parents

Last year alone, we provided over 85,000 nutritious meals and conducted health check-ups for 2,500 children, identifying and treating issues that would have otherwise gone unnoticed.

Impact Story:

Through our regular health screenings, we detected a heart condition in 8-year-old Arjun that required immediate surgery. We facilitated the procedure and after-care. Today, Arjun is healthy, active, and thriving in school.

Community Development Program

Our Community Development Program works with parents and local communities to create sustainable change that supports children's education and well-being.

Our Initiatives:

Parent education programs on the importance of schooling
Skill development and vocational training for parents
Microfinance and self-help group initiatives
Community infrastructure improvements
Advocacy for children's rights and education access

We've established 35 community centers that serve as hubs for education, skill development, and community gatherings, directly impacting over 15,000 family members.

Impact Story:

In the village of Sundarpur, we helped mothers form a tailoring cooperative that now supplies uniforms to local schools. This initiative has increased family incomes by 40% and school enrollment by 25% in the community.

Who We Are

Making a difference in the lives of children since 2010

Lit Kiran is a non-profit organization dedicated to empowering underprivileged children through education, healthcare, and community development programs. Founded in 2010, we've impacted the lives of over 10,000 children across India.

We believe that every child deserves access to quality education and opportunities, regardless of their socioeconomic background. Our holistic approach addresses not just education but also nutrition, healthcare, and community support.

Through our programs, we aim to break the cycle of poverty and create lasting change in communities across India.
